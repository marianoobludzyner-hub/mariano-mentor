# File Mapping

How interview categories map to the 5 final files. The mapping is not one-to-one - several categories feed more than one file, and one category can split across files.

## `company-context.md`

Primarily from Category 1 (Company and Stage). Include: what the company does, who it sells to, stage, tenure, customer count, ARR range, whether a CS leader exists. If a deck/website excerpt was pasted, fold its relevant facts in rather than repeating it verbatim.

Example of the right tone:
> Northwind is a logistics SaaS platform, roughly 3 years old, serving mid-market freight operators. About 60 customers today, $4M ARR. No dedicated CS leader - the founder (CEO) is still the primary point of escalation for at-risk accounts.

Not this (too transcript-like):
> Q: Tell me about your company. A: We're a logistics SaaS platform...

## `post-sales-motion-today.md`

From Category 2 (Your Post-Sales Motion Today), plus any sideways detail from other categories about tooling or process. Describe the actual motion as it runs today: what happens after close, who owns renewal and expansion, what tools exist, where it breaks down. Name the gap plainly if there's a founder-dependency pattern.

## `numbers.md`

From Category 3 (The Numbers), plus derived figures. State each number with its source ("estimated by founder" vs. "tracked in [tool]"), never presented with more certainty than it has. If NRR was derived rather than given directly, say so and show the math. Include the trend direction and the single number they named as most important.

## `team-and-operating-style.md`

From Category 4 (How the Team Operates and Decides). Cover team structure, decision-making style, AI familiarity, how early risk is typically caught, and who would credibly push back on a bad plan. This file matters most for calibrating how directive vs. collaborative the Mariano Mentor skill's advice should land.

## `goals-and-constraints.md`

From Category 5 (Goals and Constraints), plus any sideways information about past attempts or fears mentioned earlier in the interview. Cover the 6/12-month goal, what's already been tried and failed (and why, if known), the binding constraint, and what happens if nothing changes. This file is what keeps advice grounded in what's actually possible for this specific company, not generic best practice.

## General rule

If a detail could reasonably belong in two files, put the primary fact in the file it's most diagnostic for, and a one-line cross-reference in the other ("see numbers.md for the churn trend behind this"). Do not duplicate full paragraphs across files.
