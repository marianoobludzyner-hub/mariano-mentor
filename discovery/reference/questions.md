# Discovery Interview Questions

Ask one question at a time. Do not bundle several questions into one message.

---

## Category 1 - Company and Stage

1. Tell me about your company: what do you do, who do you sell to, and what stage are you at?
2. How long have you been running, and roughly how many customers do you have today?
3. What's your ARR range today, roughly? Round numbers are fine.
4. Do you have a dedicated CS or post-sales leader in place today, or is that still you?
5. If you have a deck, website copy, or company overview handy, paste the text here instead of retyping it.
6. What's the thing you're proudest of building so far?

---

## Category 2 - Your Post-Sales Motion Today

1. Walk me through what happens after a deal closes, today, start to finish.
2. Who owns renewals today? Is there one name, or does it fall to whoever notices first?
3. Who owns expansion and upsell today? Same question.
4. *(Conditional - only if there is no dedicated CS/post-sales leader)* Are you personally still the one the team escalates to, or has that moved off your plate?
5. *(Conditional - only if there is a dedicated CS leader)* How much do you still get pulled into account-level fires yourself?
6. What tools do you use for CS or post-sales today, if any - CRM, health scoring, anything else?
7. Where do you feel things are breaking down or falling through the cracks right now?

**Note on questions 4 and 5:** conditional on the answer to Category 1, question 4. If that answer is clear (there is a leader / there isn't), skip whichever of 4/5 does not apply. If it's ambiguous ("sort of," "we just hired someone"), ask the one that clarifies.

---

## Category 3 - The Numbers

Introduce this category by reminding them round numbers and honest estimates are fine, precision is not required.

1. What's your annual gross revenue churn, roughly - the percentage of ARR lost to cancellations and downgrades in the last 12 months?
2. What's your expansion revenue - upsell and cross-sell from existing customers, as a percentage of ARR, in the last 12 months?
3. Do you track NRR? If so, what is it? If not, it can be estimated from the two numbers above.
4. Has churn or NRR been trending up, down, or flat over the last year?
5. Do you know your segment mix - SMB, mid-market, enterprise? Roughly what share of ARR is each?
6. What's the one number that, if it moved, would change everything for you in the next 12 months?

---

## Category 4 - How the Team Operates and Decides

1. How is your team structured today - CS, AM, and support as one function, or separate?
2. How do you make decisions about post-sales priorities - you alone, a leadership team, gut feel, data?
3. How familiar is your team with using AI tools day to day?
4. If a big account is at risk of churning, how do you find out, and how early - weeks before, or on the renewal call itself?
5. Who on your team, if anyone, would push back if a plan like this were wrong?

---

## Category 5 - Goals and Constraints

1. Where do you want this to be in 6 months? In 12 months?
2. What have you already tried to fix this that didn't work?
3. What's the biggest obstacle standing in the way right now - budget, time, team, clarity on what to do?
4. If nothing changes, what happens to the business in a year?
5. Is there anything unusual about your situation that a generic playbook would miss?
