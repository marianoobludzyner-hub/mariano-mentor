# Team and Operating Style - Northwind Logistics (synthetic example)

Team structure: one generalist CSM covering onboarding, support, and renewals together. No separate AM or support function yet.

Decision-making: founder alone on anything post-sales related. No leadership team to weigh in - the founder is also the primary product and sales decision-maker, so post-sales competes for attention with everything else.

AI familiarity: moderate. The founder uses AI tools personally for research and writing, but nothing is deployed inside the CS workflow yet.

Risk detection today: reactive. Finds out an account is at risk when the customer raises it directly, not from any internal signal.

Who would push back on a bad plan: nobody currently, by the founder's own admission. The CSM is 8 months in and still finding their footing; there is no second senior voice in the room on post-sales decisions.
