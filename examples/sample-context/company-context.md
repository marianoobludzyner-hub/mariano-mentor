# Company Context - Northwind Logistics (synthetic example)

Northwind Logistics is a mid-market logistics SaaS platform, roughly 4 years old, serving freight and fulfillment operators across North America. About 55 customers today, $6.2M ARR. No dedicated CS or post-sales leader - the founder (CEO) is still the primary point of escalation for at-risk accounts, alongside a single generalist CSM hired 8 months ago.

Proudest builder moment cited: closing the first 10 logo deals personally in year one with no sales team, which set the tone that account relationships matter, but also set the precedent that the founder stays close to every account.

Recently acquired a smaller freight company, adding organizational complexity (a second entity, a new CFO with visibility requirements) that is starting to surface in renewal conversations.
