# Post-Sales Motion Today - Northwind Logistics (synthetic example)

After a deal closes, the CSM runs onboarding from a checklist that has not been updated since the CSM was hired. No formal handoff document from sales beyond a Slack message.

Renewals: no single owner in the formal sense. The CSM tracks renewal dates in a spreadsheet and flags them to the founder roughly 30 days out, but two of the last four renewals were flagged later than that, and one was missed entirely until the customer raised it.

Expansion: happens only when a customer directly asks about a new use case or additional seats. No proactive expansion motion exists. The founder has personally driven the only two expansion deals closed this year.

Tools: HubSpot CRM (underused for CS - mostly a sales tool historically), no dedicated health scoring, no automated alerts. The CSM keeps informal notes in Notion.

Where it's breaking down, in the founder's own words: "I only find out something's wrong when the customer is already frustrated on the call. There's no early warning."
