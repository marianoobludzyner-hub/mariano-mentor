# Goals and Constraints - Northwind Logistics (synthetic example)

6-month goal: stop being personally surprised by renewals. 12-month goal: NRR comfortably above 100% without adding CS headcount beyond the current CSM.

What's already been tried: hired the CSM 8 months ago hoping that alone would fix the visibility problem. It helped with onboarding but did not change who gets escalated to, or how early risk is caught.

Biggest obstacle, in the founder's words: "I don't actually know where to look. I just find out when it's already a problem."

If nothing changes: founder expects churn to get worse as the company scales past what one CSM and one founder can personally track, especially post-acquisition with a second entity now in the mix.

Unusual factor a generic playbook might miss: the recent acquisition adds a second entity with its own onboarding question and a new CFO who now wants visibility on renewal decisions over six figures. This is currently being handled ad hoc, not as part of any post-sales process.
